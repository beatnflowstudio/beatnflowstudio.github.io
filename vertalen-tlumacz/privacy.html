Privacy Policy: Vertalen Tłumacz

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Vertalen Tłumacz (package com.beatnflowstudio.dutch_polish_translator, the "App"), a Dutch ⇄ Polish translator by Beat & Flow Studio, handles information.

1. What we collect

The App has no accounts and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email or other personal details, and we do not store your translations on any server.

2. Text you translate

To translate, the text you type, paste or speak is sent over an encrypted connection to Google's translation service, which returns the translation. The App does not keep a history of your translations. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Voice input and read-aloud

Voice input uses your device's speech recognition service (for example Google), and read-aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine. The App only listens while you hold or tap the microphone button and does not record or store audio. Microphone permission is optional: you can always type instead.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and share device information, such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time via "Privacy settings" in the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Data stored on your device

The App only stores a small setting on your device, such as how long a rewarded "ad-free" period lasts. It is removed when you uninstall the App.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to translate and to check whether you are online.
  • Microphone (optional): only for voice input.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.

7. Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared with Google as described in sections 2 and 4.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, use your Google and Android privacy settings. If you have a question or complaint, contact us below. If you are in the EU you may also contact your national data protection authority.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
